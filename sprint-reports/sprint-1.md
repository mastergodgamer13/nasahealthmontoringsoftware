# Sprint 1 Report

## Project Topic
We chose the development of a comprehensive health management software for astronauts during space missions as our project topic.

## Research and Analysis
To establish a foundation for our idea, we analyzed previous projects from the NASA Space Apps Challenge, along with similar software solutions, websites, and existing health monitoring systems.

During our research, we noticed a recurring pattern among most of these platforms. They primarily collect astronauts' daily health data throughout their missions and generate weekly reports based on seven-day monitoring periods.

These reports evaluate different aspects of each astronaut's physical and psychological well-being, such as:
- Sleep duration
- Mood
- Blood pressure
- Other health indicators

## Identified Problem
We observed that very few of these platforms focus on providing practical recommendations or personalized solutions to help astronauts improve their daily routines, maintain their health, and preserve their psychological well-being while facing the extreme conditions and pressures of space.

## Our Project Goal
Our goal is to go beyond simply monitoring astronauts' health.

We aim to develop a system that:
- Helps astronauts maintain healthier and more stable lifestyles.
- Identifies potential early warning signs of health conditions, including infectious diseases and other medical disorders.
- Provides personalized, science-based recommendations.
- Helps astronauts reduce health risks and take preventive measures.

## Conclusion
By building upon NASA's existing research and findings in these fields, we aim to broaden the concept of astronaut health management software and create a more comprehensive, proactive, and personalized healthcare solution for future space missions.
