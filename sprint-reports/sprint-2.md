# Sprint 2 Report

## Research Phase
After finalizing our project's objectives and overall plan during Sprint 1, our team began the research phase.

We conducted research using multiple datasets available on NASA's Open Science Data Repository (OSDR). Our primary areas of interest included:

- Biological and genetic factors
- General health conditions
- Disease-related indicators
- Muscle function
- Physical stability
- Psychological well-being

## Selected Datasets

So far, we have selected and incorporated three datasets into our research: **OSD-575, OSD-569, and OSD-635.**

### OSD-575 – Blood Serum Analysis
This dataset contains blood serum data collected in connection with spaceflight research. It provides valuable information about metabolic processes, glucose levels, protein-related measurements, and other biological indicators.

These measurements can help us investigate how spaceflight affects astronauts' biological functions.

### OSD-569 – Whole-Blood Analysis
This dataset focuses on whole-blood analysis and includes information such as blood cell measurements and genomic data.

It can contribute to our understanding of biological changes associated with spaceflight and help us investigate potential health-related risks.

### OSD-635 – Vascular Smooth Muscle Cell Research
This dataset examines the effects of spaceflight on human vascular smooth muscle cells, particularly their characteristics and functions under microgravity conditions.

These findings may help us better understand how the space environment affects muscle-related biological processes.

## Prototype Development

In addition to our research, we have developed an initial prototype of our project, called **Astronaut Self-Assessment Check**, and uploaded it to our GitHub repository.

The three selected datasets have been incorporated into the prototype's code, establishing the foundation for further development.

## Next Steps

Our next objective is to:

- Improve the prototype's functionality.
- Analyze the integrated data more effectively.
- Develop a system capable of providing meaningful health assessments.
- Provide personalized recommendations for astronauts during space missions.

## Conclusion

During Sprint 2, we completed an important part of our research phase by selecting three NASA OSDR datasets and incorporating them into our initial prototype.

This work established a foundation for developing a more comprehensive astronaut health management system that combines scientific data analysis with personalized health assessments and recommendations.
